WHAT THE IPHONE REMEMBERED

53 files knew exactly where they were.

GPS and capture times reconstruct the first three days from Håverud through Duse Udde, Glava Glasbruk and into Finnskogen. Four image files arrived without location metadata and are kept in an “Unassigned” tray so they can be placed manually.
